# Intercultural Meta-Organization of Several EU-like Regional Orders

Understood. Then the target structure is **not the UN**, **not a world government**, and **not merely an assembly of states**, but rather:

> **an intercultural meta-organization made up of several EU-like regional orders**  
> that is, a kind of **civilizational federation of regional unions**.

I will call it, neutrally:

# **Intercultural Union of Regional Unions**

or, more briefly: **Meta-EU of Cultural Spheres**

It does not stand above individual countries in the same way the EU stands above its member states. It stands above **large regional-cultural spaces**, for example:

- European space: EU
- African space: African Union, plus African regional communities
- Southeast Asian space: ASEAN
- South American space: Mercosur / CELAC-like structures
- Arab-Islamic space: Arab League / OIC-like structures
- Pacific, Indian, Eurasian, Indigenous, Latin American cultural spaces, and so on

The African Union explicitly describes the integration of African states as a core aim, and it also recognizes regional economic communities as building blocks of African integration. ([au.int](https://au.int/en/overview)) ASEAN works with three community pillars: Political-Security, Economic, and Socio-Cultural Community. ([asean.org](https://asean.org/our-communities/)) Mercosur understands itself as a regional integration process in South America. ([mercosur.int](https://www.mercosur.int/en/about-mercosur/what-is-mercosur/)) The OIC describes itself as an organization with 57 member states and as the collective voice of the Muslim world. ([oic-oci.org](https://www.oic-oci.org/en))

The decisive difference from the UN:

> The UN formally orders states.  
> This imagined organization orders **cultural regions, regional unions, and civilizational spaces**.

# 56 — Topological Space of Cultural Regions

**Mapping:**  
**The world as a topological space of several cultural and regional unions.**

This is the basic level.

The EU is a regional political-legal space.  
The African Union is a pan-African integration space.  
ASEAN is a Southeast Asian cooperation and community space.  
Mercosur is a South American integration space.  
The OIC is a transregional Islamic-cultural space.  
The Arab League is an Arabic linguistic and cultural space.

The Meta-EU of cultural spheres would therefore not be a center, but a **topology of large-scale spaces**:

```text
European space
African space
Southeast Asian space
Arab-Islamic space
Latin American space
Indian space
Chinese-East Asian space
Pacific space
Indigenous spaces
Diaspora spaces
```

Important: these spaces are not cleanly separated. They overlap.

Examples:  
Morocco is African, Arab, Islamic, Mediterranean, francophone-influenced, and connected to Europe through trade.  
Indonesia is ASEAN, Islamically influenced, Pacific, Southeast Asian, and globally maritime.  
Turkey is European, Anatolian, Turkic, Islamic, NATO-adjacent, and Eurasian.

That is why **topology** is much better here than a mere map.

**56 = space of cultural spaces.**

This is not about a single organization, but about the principle:

> How can different civilizational spaces coexist without swallowing one another?

The main purpose of the Meta-EU of cultural spheres would not be to make everyone the same. Rather, it would need to create an order in which difference remains stable.

Thus:

```text
Cultural class Europe
Cultural class Africa
Cultural class Islamic world
Cultural class Latin America
Cultural class Southeast Asia
Cultural class Indian space
Cultural class Chinese-East Asian space
Cultural class Pacific
Cultural class Indigenous worlds
```

60 is therefore not “one culture above all others,” but:

> **the order in which several cultural classes occur as legitimate classes.**

This is decisive. Otherwise the organization would be imperial.

**60 = universal order of cultural classes.**

**Shared vector of several cultural regions.**

```text
Everyone becomes Western-liberal.
Everyone becomes a Chinese-authoritarian developmental state.
Everyone becomes religious.
Everyone becomes secular.
```

```text
peace without cultural erasure
development without ecological destruction
technology without dehumanization
identity without war
migration without loss of control
trade without dependency
tradition without paralysis
```

105 is therefore the **civilizational direction vector**.

Not “one truth for everyone,” but:

> a direction in which several cultures can coexist, learn, act, and mutually limit one another.
