56–111 as a Meta-Umbrella Organization of Multiple Cultures

Basic assumption

If 28–55 corresponds to a cultural umbrella organization — for example, an EU-like organization of countries within a larger cultural space — then 56–111 is not merely a “larger EU.” It corresponds to a meta-umbrella organization of several cultural umbrella organizations.

In short:

28–55 = one cultural umbrella organization of countries.
56–111 = an umbrella organization of several cultural umbrella organizations.

A better name would therefore not be “world state,” but something like:

  • federation of civilizations
  • meta-union
  • union of unions
  • intercultural operating system
  • coordination architecture of several civilizational spaces

The crucial point is: 56–111 does not primarily describe who belongs to the organization, but how this higher organization functions.

Since 2 in your scheme means frame, plurality, second level, mirroring, or systematization, the rule is:

56–111 = the frame/superstructure of 28–55.

So the question is no longer:

“How does an EU-like umbrella organization work?”

It becomes:

“How do several EU-like cultural umbrella organizations work with one another?”

Short form

56–111 corresponds to the operating logic of a meta-umbrella organization of several cultural blocs.

Its main tasks are:

  1. Map member cultures as spaces.
  2. Define interfaces between cultural blocs.
  3. Locate conflicts between cultural blocs.
  4. Create shared standards without making all cultures identical.
  5. Manage data, treaties, rules, translations, and responsibilities.
  6. Detect risks, crises, and long-term trends.
  7. Enable decisions among several major actors.
  8. Institutionalize learning, simulation, and adaptation.
  9. Produce a shared direction without forcing a single culture.

This would be an organization that manages plurality, not one that abolishes plurality.

Main insight

If 28–55 is an EU-like cultural umbrella organization, then 56–111 is no longer an ordinary political organization.

It is closer to:

a formal coordination machine for several civilizational spaces.

It works only if five conditions are met at the same time:

  1. It has shared rules.
  2. It does not destroy the distinctness of cultures.
  3. It recognizes differences semantically and institutionally.
  4. It can model conflicts, risks, and trends.
  5. It has hard limits against abuse of power.

The shortest formula is:

56–111 = meta-governance of several cultural umbrella organizations.

Or more strongly:

56–111 = the operating system of a plural world order.
