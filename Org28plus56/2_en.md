## Most important correction within your mapping

**37 and 38 should remain separate.**  
37 is data and model management.  
38 is the optimization of that management and of legal/policy processes.

**40 should be called “variety” rather than “manifold” if you mean social classes.**  
“Manifold” fits better if you mean local charts, transitions, and overlaps. “Variety” fits better if you mean several structured classes as one composite thing.

**51 is right as a candidate, but not as pure Bayesian statistics.**  
The EU is not a Bayesian machine. It is more of a deliberative evidence machine: statistics, expert opinions, interests, risks, legal principles, and political power are condensed into a decidable position.

**55 is very strong.**  
If 50 is machine learning as a learning process, then 55 is AI as generator: a system produces outputs that causally intervene in society, administration, economy, and law.
