# Why Does This Stacked Truth-Value Currency Lead to These Dimensions?

## Question

Why does this stacked truth-value currency lead to these dimensions, such as causality?

## Answer

Because this “currency” does **not measure value**. It measures **the truth of action**.

A normal money currency roughly answers:

```text
How much is something worth?
How much can someone pay?
How much is exchanged?
```

A stacked truth-value currency answers instead:

```text
Is the need real?
What is the cause?
How urgent is it?
How strong is it?
Which means exist?
What effect will emerge?
Is the action pointed in the right direction?
```

That is why dimensions such as **causality** emerge. As soon as an economy is no longer steered by price, quantity, and ownership, it must know **why** something is being done and **whether** the action actually touches the cause.

## The Core

In your system, an economic action is not a purchase such as:

```text
I buy commodity X for price Y.
```

It is closer to:

```text
A real state is changed.
```

So:

```text
state before → action → state after
```

For that to work, the system needs dimensions. Every action has several truth questions.

For example:

```text
Hunger exists.
But why?
```

Possible causes:

```text
too little food
bad distribution
missing refrigeration
broken transport routes
drought
poverty
war
wrong planning
food waste
```

If the system only sees “hunger = high,” it can act wrongly.

But if it measures **causality**, it asks:

```text
Does this action touch the cause of hunger?
```

That is why causality becomes its own dimension.

## Why Not Just One Single Truth Value?

Because one single value would be too crude.

If you compress everything into one number, something similar to money happens again:

```text
everything becomes comparable,
but many things become invisible.
```

Price makes many things invisible:

```text
destruction of nature
care work
urgency
the real cause
social consequences
future damage
local particularities
power relations
```

The stacked truth value is meant to prevent exactly that.

That is why it is not:

```text
truth = 87 points
```

but:

```text
truth = [causality, time, intensity, existence, potencies, effects,
         substance, matter, difference, determination, phenomena, angle direction]
```

So it is a stack.

Not one value.  
It is a **multi-layered signature of reality**.

## Example: Causality

Suppose a commune has homelessness.

Without a causality dimension, the system might say:

```text
housing need high → build new houses
```

But perhaps the cause is not missing housing space. It may be:

```text
vacancy
wrong allocation
rents that are too high
lack of renovation
speculation
social exclusion
missing accessibility
```

Then new construction may not be the right action.

With causality, the system asks:

```text
Does new construction really help?
Or does vacancy use help?
Or renovation?
Or redistribution?
Or social support?
```

The dimension **causality** therefore prevents blind activism.

It says:

```text
Not every effect is the right effect.
An action is only good if it touches the cause.
```

## Why Exactly These Dimensions?

Because every economic action needs at least these questions.

| Dimension | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| **Causality** | Does the action touch the cause or only a symptom? |
| **Time** | Must action happen immediately or later? |
| **Intensity** | How strong is the need, damage, or effect? |
| **Existence** | Does the problem really exist, or is it only being claimed? |
| **Potencies** | Are there abilities, means, and possibilities for solving it? |
| **Effects** | What does the action change in the system? |
| **Substance** | Which materials, energy, food, care time, or resources are available? |
| **Matter** | Where is the real infrastructure or resource located? |
| **Difference** | How large is the gap between need and reality? |
| **Determination** | Is the action socially, democratically, and meaningfully determined? |
| **Phenomena** | How does the state show itself visibly, measurably, or experientially? |
| **Angle direction** | Does the action work regeneratively, neutrally, harmfully, or controllingly? |

These dimensions arise because a planetary economy must not only know:

```text
What does it cost?
```

It must know:

```text
What is happening?
Why is it happening?
How urgent is it?
What can be done?
Which means are available?
What consequences will emerge?
Is it planetarily viable?
Is it socially legitimate?
```

## The “Currency” Is Therefore Not Purchasing Power

If you still call it a “currency,” then it is a different kind of currency.

Not:

```text
money currency = claim to purchase
```

but:

```text
truth-value currency = claim to attention, priority, and action
```

Or:

```text
money asks: who can pay?
the truth stack asks: what is really necessary and right?
```

That is the decisive difference.

## Why Causality Cannot Be Replaced by Effect

One might ask:

```text
Is the dimension effect not enough?
```

No.

An action can have an effect and still touch the wrong cause.

Health example:

```text
Painkillers have an effect.
But if the cause is poisoned water,
the water problem must be solved.
```

So:

```text
effect = what happens?
causality = does it touch the origin?
```

Both are necessary.

## Why Causality Cannot Be Replaced by Difference

Difference is also not enough.

```text
difference = something is missing.
causality = why is it missing?
```

Food example:

```text
difference high: people have too little food.
```

But the cause could be:

```text
drought
transport failure
lack of storage
unfair distribution
soil destruction
seed shortage
energy failure
```

Depending on the cause, a different action is needed.

That is why causality is its own dimension.

## Why Causality Cannot Be Replaced by Democracy

Determination is also not enough.

A majority can want something and still be causally wrong.

Example:

```text
Many people want more roads against traffic jams.
```

But the cause of congestion may be:

```text
sprawl
missing public transport
wrong work schedules
bad urban planning
```

Then more road construction can even increase congestion over the long term.

So:

```text
determination = is it socially legitimated?
causality = is it factually right with respect to the cause?
```

Both must come together.

## The Stacked Truth Value Creates Contract Conditions

The dimensions are not decoration. They change the action.

Example:

```text
causality low
time high
intensity high
```

This means:

```text
it is urgent and strong,
but the cause is unclear.
```

Then the contract should not be executed blindly in full. Instead:

```text
immediate emergency help
parallel cause investigation
later correction duty
```

Another example:

```text
difference high
substance low
angle direction low
```

This means:

```text
the need is real,
means are scarce,
and the planned action has harmful effects.
```

Then:

```text
do not simply release it
search for an alternative
check repair / conversion / redistribution
```

## The Most Important Point

The dimensions emerge because your system wants to **replace trading/action as buying and selling**.

Buying and selling used to be very simple codes:

```text
buying = I take something for money.
selling = I give something for money.
```

In your system this becomes:

```text
buying = accepting a necessary effect.
selling = contributing ability, time, substance, or effect.
```

But then one must know:

```text
Which effect?
Why?
When?
How strong?
Made of what?
Where?
For whom?
With which consequences?
In which direction?
```

Exactly from that, the dimensions arise.
