# Trade in dimensions: contract, truth stack, effect

This protocol shows how buying, selling, import, export, labour market, product market, and service market are replaced in the simulation.

**Old form:** commodity + quantity + price + ownership → purchase/sale

**New form:** causality + time + intensity + existence + potencies + effects + substance + matter + difference + determination + phenomena + angle direction → effect contract

The stacked truth value is the twelve-dimensional state of a trade. Each dimension lies on 0..4. The base-five stack is one digit per dimension; the score is not money but a priority and validity number.

## Dimensions

| short | dimension | contract question | economic replacement |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | Causality | Does the action touch the real cause, or only a symptom? | replaces price signal with cause and effect proof |
| T | Time | How urgent is the effect, and in what time window must it arrive? | replaces delivery date with real urgency |
| I | Intensity | How strong are need, damage, burden, or positive effect? | replaces willingness to pay with strength of the phenomenon |
| E | Existence | Is the phenomenon present, measured, reported, and checkable? | replaces ownership proof with proof of state existence |
| P | Potencies | Which abilities, reserves, tools, and possible paths exist? | replaces return on capital with real capacity for transformation |
| F | Effects | Which positive and negative system consequences arise? | replaces profit with real effects on people, nature, and infrastructure |
| S | Substance | Which matter, energy, food, water, knowledge, or care time is available? | replaces commodity value with material condition |
| M | Matter | Where are the material place and infrastructure, and can they be reached? | replaces market access with real material location |
| D | Difference | How large is the gap between need and existing reality? | replaces demand with need gap |
| V | Determination | Is the action socially decided, contestable, and meaningful? | replaces partner power with collective determination |
| N | Phenomena | How does the state appear in observation, measurement, and affected reports? | replaces market observation with appearance and feedback logic |
| R | Angle direction | In which direction does the action move: regenerative, neutral, exploitative, or controlling? | replaces growth direction with effect direction |

| governance | Determination effect: decision, law, conflict resolution, rights, truth correction | rules, minutes, decision registers, privacy tools, audit reports, conflict procedures | moderators; legal workers; privacy stewards; citizen councils; mediators; auditors; ombuds offices | participation, objection procedures, truth review, resource release, conflict resolution | ecological limits are publicly watched and must not be voted away / climate contracts need transparency, long horizons, and rights against displacement or technocracy |
| knowledge | Knowledge effect: research, open plans, diagnosis, simulation, learning curves | open plans, measurement data, models, teaching material, build guides, diagnostic protocols | researchers; data stewards; simulation workers; librarians; technology transfer workers; local learning workshops | analysis, advice, development, documentation, knowledge transfer, error review | knowledge must reveal substance consequences and must not hide externalisation / climate models, adaptation knowledge, and technology assessment are core content |

| repair | Repair effect: lifetime, reuse, spare part recovery, capacity preservation | spare parts, restored devices, repaired clothing, building parts, tool sets, recovered material | repair workshops; circular designers; sorters; maintainers; electronic, textile, and building repairers | repair, maintenance, diagnosis, refurbishment, material recovery, lifetime extension | reduces waste, raw material pressure, and toxins; toxin safety remains a condition / lowers embodied emissions and material throughput; energy heavy repair must be ecologically worthwhile |
| ecology | Regeneration effect: soil, biodiversity, water balance, cooling, habitat | restoration land, seed, wetlands, agroforestry, urban green, soil protection material | ecologists; restoration crews; forest and agroforestry keepers; waterway carers; biodiversity monitors | soil building, rewetting, afforestation, species monitoring, waterway restoration, cooling area planning | this is direct ecological basic function; exploitation must not be booked as regeneration / binding, cooling, water retention, and adaptation raise angle direction and effect |
| waste | Waste difference resolution: sorting, toxin securing, return, composting | secondary materials, compost, sorted metals, plastics, safe landfill units, repair material | sorters; recyclers; toxin testers; composters; circular logisticians; material auditors | collection, separation, decontamination, recovery, reuse, safe final storage | toxins must stay out of soil, water, and bodies; cycle before landfill / methane, burning, transport, and avoided new production set climate effect |
